Tsuyama to Fukuoka<

Tsuyama and Okayama

Today the plan was to move to Tsuyama, which is a small town in central Japan, due to the mountains in the area, there wasnt any trains taking me directly there, so instead i had to get a coach. I had booked a ticket to tsuyama the day before, for what i thought was noon, but once i got there, it turns out it was arriving at tsuyama at noon 😅. So i talked to the ticket desk, and they allowed me anyways onto the bus, which i was very grateful for. The timetable did say it would take about 3hours, but due to heavy traffic in Osaka it was closer to 4 and a half. so i arried in Tsuyama at 1630, which is unfortunate since all the toursity places shut at 17:00, and since it takes about 20mins to walk to the hotel there was no chance of doing that today, so the plans were pushed to tomorrow. Instead i checked into the hotel, turns out i was the only person here, the hotel itself is really modern and clean, which was nice. Instead of seeing the castle i took a walk into the centre, and wandered around, i took a detour to a park that was nearby, since that was kinda the only point of interest that was still open. But by that point it was getting late, so i headed back towards the center from the gardens in search of food. I ended up at a small restaurant, that was very unasuming on the outside. But the food was so good. I started with gyozas, but instead of the normal dough wrapping it was chicken skin, which was an interesting change to the normal, aswell as crispy chicken. Due to the translation of the menu, i overestimated the size, so i asked the waiter for what they recomend, and they talked about a Hot pot style meal. So thats what i had, It is very similair to a hot pot, except its already been cooked, so you have all the ingreadients allready ready to eat. That was really nice and fresh, and i had no complaints there. After i walked home, and decided that since this hotel had a bath, i might aswell try it out. Being Japan though it took me about 10minutes to figure out what the 15 different buttons, knobs, switches and screens do. But eventually i figured it out and relaxed, ready for the next day.

The next day i woke up bright and early to do some exploring of the town again. I first checked out and lugged my suitase back ot the station to leave it at a coin locker. This is something i feel is under appreciated in Japan, and that is that every station has coin lockers, which makes it so much easier if you have a stop over, and its cheap! So i left that and walked over to the castle, along the way i stopped by a small hole in the wall place that was selling red and white bean filled pancakes, for only 100 yean each, it was run by an elderly couple and i had to try one of each, this bean paste is very common in japan, and even if it is a little savoury, its soething that you will find on anything to make it a dessert.As i munched on that i walked over to the castle, which is actually more of a ruin where they rebuilt one of the turrets to house some info about the castle. It had all the usual history and how it was important in the civil war in the 17th century, but the views of the town were the best around. Not far from the castle Tsuyama also had a Museum, which went over the history of Tsuyama and what happened in the area, aswell as many statues and replicas of ancient artifacts, aswell as a dinosaur :) But overall it was quite average. Afterwards i decided its worth going to find some lunch, and nearby there was a small cafe, run by some pensioners, they were really sweet and tried to help me the best they could with their lack of english, but i ordered a crouquette meal, and it was massive, and i really struggled to finish it, but it was really good, and i couldnt dissapoint them, so i powered through, along with a coffee, it cost me only 1200yen.
It was getting to around 2pm at this point, so i decided its worth heading back, and if i did i would comfortably catch the next train to Okayama. The train arrived and it was really old train, with what felt like table fans strapped to the ceiling as AC, but the seats were soft and the nature pretty, so i didnt really complain, and the journy was over before i knew it. Okayama is a much more modern and youthful city, and with me staying at a hostel just 10mins away from the central station, i was surronded by hip stores and thrifting places, so i spent a while looking through them as i walked over the Okayama castle, which was about 3km away. Originall the plan was t do the castle and the gardens on the same day, but i only had time for 1, so i did the castle. This castle is unique for its black exterior and unique panelling, which made it look very mysterious. But it had been completely rebuilt in the 60s, so it was again made as a museum, but the exhibit was actually quite interesting, there was a very famour battle that happneded here between 3 generals, where one backstabbed the eastern army during the civil war, that turned the tide in the war. But they went into detail about each general, their motives and past life, to get a really good feel of how it woldve been like to be one of them, it was very imersive and interesting way of doing it.
Once the castle closed i began the alk home and decided to have dinner in the hostel as they had some intersting things on the menu. So i went at sat at the bar (as it was the only place available) and i ended up chatting with a local and a french guy thats in okayam for en exchange, it was very interesting to talk to them and i had a nice night with them. But eventually i deicided its probably best to get a good nights sleep before travelling to fukuoka. So thats what i did
